Building a risk index that behaves like a setlist

Nov 2024 · Finance

A good setlist balances intensity. I used that same logic to build a risk index—stacking scenarios like songs so investors feel the pacing.

Each scenario was labeled as opener, mid-show, or closer. Openers introduce volatility gently; closers stress the system to see if it holds. Repeats mimic choruses: the same risk appearing across multiple time frames to test endurance.

Risk should feel like dynamics, not surprises—loud sections framed by intentional silence.

In the UI, this became a timeline with three rules:

  • Thin spines and dots to show sequence without heavy boxes.
  • Ghost-outline buttons to invite interaction without shouting.
  • One accent color to mark commitments, never decoration.

The finished tool reads like a show plan: you can see what’s coming, how long it lasts, and where to take a breath. That familiarity makes risk less abstract and more actionable.
