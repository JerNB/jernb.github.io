Mapping Stillness

6 min read Dec 2024 Whitespace, pacing

I map products the way I map walking routes: with pauses. Stillness is a design material; it decides how long the eye rests before it continues.

I begin with pacing diagrams—bars for rhythm, dots for breaths. Each interaction sits on the map. Does this modal interrupt or accompany? Does the scroll feel like a drift or a sprint?

Whitespace is space with responsibility. It holds the narrative together.

Typography carries the load. I pair one display weight with a calm, readable body, then let color do the whispering. The accent is never louder than the sentence it supports.

Motion is restrained to entry and exit. Elements slide into place like pages turning in a book: a hint of rotation, a soft shadow, then stillness. The product breathes with you, not at you.
