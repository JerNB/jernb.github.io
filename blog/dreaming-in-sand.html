Dreaming in Sandboxes

5 min read Feb 2025 Prototyping, motion

I build first drafts inside loose, glitchy sandboxes. There are no grids, no rules about color, just shapes and timings colliding until something feels like a heartbeat worth keeping.

Prototyping is dreamwork for interfaces—abstract, soft, and playful before it hardens into production.

I start with a single constraint: everything must move with purpose. A button should lean into you before it responds. A panel should dim just enough that your eyes rest. These micro-decisions become a language.

Once I see a pattern, I codify it. Tokens for motion curves. Tokens for glow, shadow, and depth. The sandbox becomes a library; the dream becomes a system.

  • Sketch with contrasts: sharp blocks beside soft blur fields.
  • Animate in layers: color shift first, then depth, then subtle scale.
  • Keep verbs simple: hover, breathe, fade, settle.

The final interface carries a memory of the sandbox—nothing is overly polished; it feels alive. That balance between control and spontaneity is what keeps products warm.
