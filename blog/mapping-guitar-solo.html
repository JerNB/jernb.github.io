Mapping a guitar solo into data

Jan 2025 · Data, Music

Bends, slides, and sustain already contain structure. I charted a solo from our latest set to see how its dynamics could inform calmer dashboards.

The first step was treating every phrase as a segment with duration and intensity. Sustain became a plateau; quick hammer-ons became spikes. When visualized, the line looked like a cautious heartbeat—exactly the pacing a financial product needs when showing risk.

Interfaces can borrow the language of sound: volume maps to visual weight, tempo maps to update cadence.

In practice, I translated each musical gesture into UI motion:

  • Long sustains → slower progress indicators that steady the eye.
  • Vibrato → micro color shifts that suggest activity without shouting.
  • Rests → intentional whitespace to let users breathe between actions.

The result is a dashboard that feels less like a ticker and more like a well-paced setlist—predictable, calm, and expressive when it needs to be.
