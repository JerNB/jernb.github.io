Agency Is All You Need

Oct 20, 2026 · AI, Learning, Building
← All essays 阅读中文原文 →

AI empowers everyone. But what it amplifies is always the person using it.

I want to share a few thoughts on AI.

I think AI is an incredibly powerful tool, one that empowers everyone. But at its core, it enables and amplifies; the outcome depends on the person using it. This came up in a conversation I had with a professor over a Flunch meal.

For someone with ability and direction, AI can multiply what they are capable of. On the other hand, if someone lacks the skills and does not understand the underlying logic or concepts, using AI will only create dependence.

That is why I think agency matters even more in the AI era: how a person chooses to use the tool, ideally with a strong sense of purpose.

Take me, for example. I do not think I am particularly smart, and I certainly have not fully figured out AI. But my agency shows up in my willingness to spend time exploring, working on projects, and turning ideas into action.

AI has already made it possible for us to do things we could not do before: build a website, an app, or a small tool. What matters even more, though, is how we learn and how we use it.

With those possibilities now within reach, I think this is the best time for learning through experience. If you are willing to put in the time, you can build just about anything, and learn the principles behind it along the way.

That brings me to a skill that has been getting a lot of attention recently: REA (Reverse Engineer Anything). It gives AI the ability to reverse-engineer all kinds of things, and I have been using it throughout the past week.

I have always been interested in music. Previously, I studied how models could predict a song's popularity and built a music recommendation system. This time, I used REA to take apart GuitarTuna and learn about the algorithms behind it: how sound is converted into frequency, how frequency is translated into cents, and technologies such as AudioWorklet that I had never worked with before.

Then, building on what I had learned, I made a tuner of my own: six-string-tuner.

The whole process was fun and rewarding. I had made something that worked, and learned something in the process.

AI amplifies who you already are. Agency determines who you want to become.

There has never been a better time to learn through experience.

Agency is all you need.
