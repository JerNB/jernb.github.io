Jerry Wu · vocalist, data and design

Blending stage, spreadsheets, and screens.

I sing for Six Drowners and write songs that travel between noise and stillness. Offstage, I build risk-aware finance tools and design interfaces that prefer clarity over spectacle.

I borrow structure from data, rhythm from music, and atmosphere from visual design. Whether it’s a setlist or a roadmap, I think in pacing, dynamic range, and the small gestures that keep people engaged without being loud.

When collaborating, I move from sketches to quick prototypes, then tighten the details—type, timing, and how each click feels. I like work that is calm on the surface but precise underneath.

“Music teaches me pacing; data teaches me consequence. Design is where both become choices people can feel.”
