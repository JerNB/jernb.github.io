Quiet work, loud meaning

Hi! I'm Jerry.

My Chinese name is Yanrui Wu. I grew up in China, and I'm heading to Duke with the class of 2030.

I spend most of my time moving between music, data, and interfaces. I sing for Six Drowners, write songs, build small tools, and keep notes on whatever refuses to leave my head.

I like things that have structure but still breathe: a good setlist, a course map, a recommendation system, a page that feels quiet until you get closer.

Now

  • Building Duke Stats Major Atlas, a student-facing map for courses, tracks, prerequisites, and the weird little decisions that shape a statistics major.
  • Working on a song recommendation project: part taste diary, part recommender experiment, part attempt to explain why certain songs find you at the right time.
  • Still updating my public writing / WeChat notes, usually about music, data, school, and the strange ways people make decisions.

I've Built

  • Duke Stats Major Atlas — a practical guide for navigating the statistics major before the choices become stressful.
  • Song rec project — a recommendation experiment built around taste, context, and the language people use when they talk about music.
  • StatsifyFinance — an earlier finance-learning site about making risk visible. Archived in spirit, even if the lessons still matter.
  • School Business & Economics Club — sessions and frameworks that teach how to think, not what to think
  • YouMe — a student live-house network helping bands perform in real venues
  • Music ML research — Predicting Hit Songs, an exploratory study on audio features and model tradeoffs (published in Scholarly Review)

I Care About

Texture over noise Craft over speed Clarity without losing emotion Systems that respect people Risk that is explained, not hidden
