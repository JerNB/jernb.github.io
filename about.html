Hi! I'm Jerry.

My full Chinese Name is Yanrui Wu. I'm a senior from a high school in China and an incoming Duke class of 2030.

I build risk-aware finance tools and interfaces that move like music. I sing and write original songs with Six Drowners.

I borrow structure from data, rhythm from music, and atmosphere from visual design. Whether it's a setlist or a roadmap, I think in pacing, dynamic range, and small gestures that keep people engaged without being loud.
