Acoustic Night – School Auditorium

School Auditorium 2025 Acoustic set

We stripped the arrangements down to vocals, nylon strings, and light percussion. The auditorium’s natural reverb gave the set a soft halo without extra effects.

The setlist borrowed from our louder shows but slowed the pace, letting lyrics and pauses carry the room. Audience claps became part of the rhythm.

We recorded room mics to study how much space a quiet set can hold; it’s shaping how we write the next batch of songs.
