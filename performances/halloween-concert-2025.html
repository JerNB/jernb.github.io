Halloween concert

Live 2025 · 10 · 31 Night set

We played two originals and let the room breathe between them—big choruses, heavy reverb, and a crowd that stayed with us. The atmosphere felt electric, every silhouette moving in sync with the backline glow.

We also covered Smashing Pumpkins’ “Today.” Great effect, great song, and it landed perfectly in the middle of the set.

The night closed with a slow build into noise, then a clean drop to silence. Perfect Halloween pacing.
