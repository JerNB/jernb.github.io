First studio session

Recording 2025 Session A

We tracked vocals and guitars live in one room to capture the raw edges before layering synths. The session became a blueprint for how we pace future recordings—tight takes, generous breaks.

Gear was pared down: one condenser, one dynamic, and a shared headphone mix. Keeping the setup minimal let the songs breathe like they do on stage.
