Six Drowners: Winter Session

Winter 2024 Studio session Pre-EP tracking

We booked a winter block to track vocals and guitars live before overdubbing synths. The focus was on capturing honest takes without over-polish.

To keep decisions fast, we limited ourselves to two takes per section and used minimal processing. The room tone became part of the character we kept in the final mix.

These recordings informed our live dynamics: when to lean into noise, when to keep space for breath.
