Live at Shenzhen – Summer 2024

Shenzhen Summer 2024 Live-house

We opened with a slow build, then locked into the setlist arc that mirrors our favorite live albums. Shenzhen’s crowd gave us room to stretch the solos and test new transitions.

Lighting was minimal—mostly white strobes and shadows—to keep the focus on the dynamics. The encore leaned into the heavier tracks before ending on a clean, quiet outro.

Backstage, we captured board mixes and crowd mics to study pacing for future tours.
